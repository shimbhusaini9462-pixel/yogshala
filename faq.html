Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your practice with Yogshala.

I am new to yoga. Can I join?

Yes. Yogshala offers practice for beginners and different experience levels. Tell the instructor about your experience and goals so the team can suggest a suitable class.

Which types of yoga classes are available?

Choose centre classes, live online yoga, personal sessions or teacher training. Weekly, ten-class, couple/group and home-visit options are listed on our Classes & Fee Structure page.

What are the class timings and holidays?

The online Morning Yoga batch is scheduled for 7?8 AM IST, Monday?Saturday, with Sundays and listed festival holidays off, through 31 December 2026. View the online schedule. Centre batch timings are on the Classes page; confirm your chosen batch before booking.

Where can I check prices and package validity?

Select Indian or International pricing on the package page. Weekly packages are valid for 7 consecutive days with up to 6 group sessions; ten-class packages have a suggested validity of 45 days. Proposed prices require confirmation when you enquire.

How do I book a class or package?

Complete Join Our Classes with your client type, package and yoga type. After submission, the WhatsApp message opens; press Send in WhatsApp to notify Yogshala. Advance booking or an appointment is required for the listed packages.

Can I pay cash or pay at the centre?

Yes. Select Cash or Offline Payment (Pay at Centre) in the enquiry form. No screenshot is needed. Your enquiry can be submitted successfully, while payment remains pending until received and verified.

Is a screenshot required for online payment?

Yes. UPI, bank transfer, international bank transfer and PayPal submissions require a JPG, PNG or WebP screenshot up to 2 MB. The screenshot is saved for admin review; submission does not automatically confirm payment or activate membership.

How do I join a live online class?

Register once, then log in to your student dashboard. Admin confirms payment and assigns your batch. When your teacher starts the class, an eligible student sees Join Live Class. The video opens inside the website; students do not need a separate Jitsi account.

What do I need for an online class?

Use a stable internet connection and a phone or computer with a camera and microphone. Keep a yoga mat, comfortable clothing and any instructor-recommended props ready. Allow camera/microphone permissions when prompted.

What happens if the teacher has not started, or I miss a class?

Your scheduled time remains visible in the dashboard. Wait for your teacher to start; a waiting video may play if one has been added by Yogshala. Classes are live and are not recorded. Contact the team about the next suitable session if you miss a class.

Are private, home-visit and hotel classes different?

Yes. Private centre sessions are one-to-one and separate from long-term Personal Yoga memberships. Home visits cover selected Jaipur areas, subject to availability and possible travel charges. Home pricing is proposed; the confirmed Hotel Yoga rates are separate. Therapy sessions require instructor suitability assessment.

I forgot my student password. What should I do?

Choose Forgot password? on the Student Login page and enter the email used for registration. Check your inbox and spam folder for the one-use reset link, valid for 30 minutes. Contact Yogshala if you need help.

Still have a question? Chat with Yogshala on WhatsApp or contact our team.
